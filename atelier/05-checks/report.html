Frantz portal: how it works for new and existing customers

This is the state of the prototype before any fixes. I walked the main flows as five personas, did a 5-second clarity test and stress-tested the pages. I compared everything with the interviews in Jamie. Nothing has been changed in the prototype.

    The six things that matter most

    The details below fall into these themes. Fixing them would solve most of the P0 and P1 findings.

    1. "Paste, upload, done" is not safe yet

      Existing customers paste their whole agreed strategy. The prototype quietly replaces the period, the pause and the channel split with a default. Finished ads hide behind the third «Endre». This is the only P0.

    2. "Let Frantz choose" stops halfway

      Hjem promises «så fyller Frantz ut resten». With only a link, budget and channels show «Mangler», and «Bruk anbefalt» fills the budget but no channels. A new customer must choose alone.

    3. The moment of ordering is unclear

      Next to «Bestill kampanjen», nothing says that Frantz checks the order, when it is binding or what the money buys. The good explanation appears only after ordering.

    4. Dialogue still leaves the portal

      «Be om endring», «Kontakt rådgiver» and the assistant are three routes, and two open an email. Users told us that two routes already confuse them.

    5. The manager approves without seeing an ad

      The approval loop works well. But when there is no ad material, the manager sees only price and channels, not a preview.

    6. Numbers have no context

      The report is clear in its words. But no benchmark says whether 1,5 % or kr 1 730 per «Søk» is good. No forecast is shown before ordering.

    Two groups, two different problems

    Every person we interviewed already uses business.frantz.no. So the new-customer view is based on inference, not interviews.

    New customers

    • Hjem explains Frantz well: «Kom i gang» and «Hva er en kampanje?» use no product names.
    • The tour opens on top of «Lag første kampanje» and hides the main button.
    • They are asked for budget and channels they cannot judge.
    • Nothing at the order button explains review, binding or invoice.
    • «Sporingskode» and «Bruk lagret pakke» show up with no explanation.

    Existing customers moving from today's portal

    • Search by their own reference works. This was a top request.
    • Pasted strategies lose period and channel split without a warning.
    • Uploading finished ads takes 4 extra steps in a modal.
    • They get the same «Start en kampanje» tour as a new customer. Nothing says what has changed or where things moved.
    • Dialogue still goes to email, with three entry points.

    What already works

    • Find by own referenceA search for «WN-4471» found the campaign at once. The reference leads the title in the list and on the page.
    • The approval loopNo login, one click to approve, «Å åpne lenken godkjenner ingenting», and the sender sees «Godkjent» with the next step.
    • No product names«Autopublish», «Tailored» and «Frantz AI» never appear for a new customer.
    • Plain report words«Trykket «Søk»» instead of CTR, and the adviser note explains what Frantz did.
    • The 19.10 fixes holdPaid extras are suggested, not ticked. «Totalt eks. mva.» shows before ordering.
    • Solid on phonesNo page scrolls sideways at 320–1920 px. 200% zoom holds. Ola copied the report link in 4 taps.

    Gaps in what we know

    1. No interview tested this prototype. The 23.10 interviews were about today's portal.
    2. No first-time customer was interviewed. The new-customer findings are inferred.
    3. No end client or manager who approves was interviewed.
    4. Benchmarks, cancel rules and invoice timing must come from Frantz.
    5. 8 external users, several of them partners or part-owners of Frantz.
