# Judge my design — Frantz portal, 03.11.2026

I have never seen this product. I play a new customer: a recruiter at Nordlys Rekruttering AS who wants to advertise a job. I assume the portal is for both new and existing customers.

Path I walked: Logg inn → Hjem → Ny kampanje (demo «Lenke og tekst») → summary → «Bestill kampanjen» → campaign page → Kampanjer → Stillinger → Tilbudskalkulator → Lagrede pakker.

---

## 1. Logg inn

**What I saw:** «Ny hos Frantz? Kontakt oss, så setter vi opp en konto.» Can a new customer only get in by talking to someone?
**Why it matters:** A new customer who wants to try it now hits a wall on screen one.
**What to change:** Keep it, but make the contact a real link or button: «Ny hos Frantz? Be om en konto».
**Tag:** test it

## 2. Hjem

**What I saw:** «Venter på dere» sits under three dark number tiles. Why do the numbers come first, when the list is the thing I must act on?
**Why it matters:** A regular wants to know «what do I need to do today». The tiles push that down.
**What to change:** Move «Venter på dere» to the top, tiles under it. Variant B only.
**Tag:** test it

**What I saw:** «Trykket «Søk»» as a headline number. What is «Søk» to me? Did people search, or apply?
**Why it matters:** The best number on the page uses an internal word. The help («Hva betyr «Søk»?») is far down, on another card.
**What to change:** Tile label «Trykket på «Søk»-knappen» or «Gikk videre til søknaden». Put the help link next to the tile.
**Tag:** test it

**What I saw:** «Vis meg rundt» is a small text link in the intro.
**Why it matters:** This is the only first-timer help on Hjem, and it is easy to miss.
**What to change:** Show it as a dismissable card only on first login.
**Tag:** test it

## 3. Ny kampanje — step 1

**What I saw:** Three ways to start: «Hent fra Stillinger», a link, and a free-text box. Plus «Bruk utfyllingsmal». What is the difference? Which one should I pick?
**Why it matters:** Four doors on the first step. A new customer does not know what «Stillinger» is yet.
**What to change:** Lead with one field: «Lim inn lenke til stillingsannonsen». Put «Hent fra Stillinger» and free text under «Eller …».
**Tag:** test it

**What I saw:** The button says «Fyll ut skjemaet». Who fills it? Me or the system?
**Why it matters:** The label does not say that the system reads my text and builds the plan. I am not sure what happens at the click.
**What to change:** «Lag forslag til kampanje» or «Neste: se forslaget».
**Tag:** test it

## 4. Ny kampanje — summary

**What I saw:** I wrote «Vi vil ha strategisk rådgivning». The page found it, but left the box **unticked**: «Dere nevnte dette i teksten. Kryss av hvis dere vil ha det.»
**Why it matters:** I asked for it. Now I must ask again. If I miss it, I think I ordered it, and I did not. That is a broken promise on the most important screen.
**What to change:** Tick it for me, and say «Lagt til fordi dere nevnte det. Fjern hvis dere ikke vil ha det.» The price goes into the total at once.
**Tag:** no-brainer (but confirm with Frantz that pre-ticking a paid extra is OK)

**What I saw:** My own text shows twice: at the top and again in «Tilleggsinformasjon».
**Why it matters:** I think I must write more, or that something got copied by mistake.
**What to change:** Show the text once. Keep «Tilleggsinformasjon» empty, with placeholder «Noe mer Frantz bør vite?».
**Tag:** no-brainer

**What I saw:** «Strategisk rådgivning +kr 8 000» sits right above «Bestilling» and the price. Does help always cost 8 000?
**Why it matters:** The paid extra and the free «Meld interesse»-knapp are in the same box. A new user can read the box as «this is what the campaign costs with help».
**What to change:** Keep the label «Valgfritt», and add one line under the heading: «Kampanjen fungerer uten disse.»
**Tag:** test it

**What I saw:** Two buttons at the end: «Send planen til godkjenning» and «Bestill kampanjen». What is the difference? What do I miss if I pick the other?
**Why it matters:** I wrote «Kunden må godkjenne annonsen». So which one is right for me? Approval of the **plan** and approval of the **ads** are two things, and the page uses both.
**What to change:** If my text mentions approval, make «Send planen til godkjenning» the primary button. Rename to «Send planen til sjefen eller kunden».
**Tag:** test it

**What I saw:** «Bestill kampanjen». Is this binding? Does money go now?
**Why it matters:** The page says «Oppstart avklares med Frantz» and «Frantz går gjennom bestillingen». So the click is a request, not a purchase. The label is scarier than what happens.
**What to change:** Test «Send bestillingen til Frantz» and the line «Ingenting publiseres før dere har godkjent annonsene.» right above the button.
**Tag:** test it

**What I saw:** At 1440 px wide, the form takes the left half and the right half is empty.
**Why it matters:** The price is at the very bottom. I scroll a long way before I see what I pay.
**What to change:** Put «Prisoversikt» and the two buttons in a sticky order card on the right.
**Tag:** test it

**What I saw:** «Lagrede pakker» says «Velg en pakke i «Ny kampanje»». I did not see a package choice anywhere in my path.
**Why it matters:** A promise on one page that the other page does not keep.
**What to change:** Show the saved packages on the summary under «Kampanjen», or change the text on Lagrede pakker.
**Tag:** test it

## 5. After the order — campaign page

**What I saw:** I wrote «Vi har ferdig materiell». First thing on the campaign page: a yellow «Annonsene mangler» with «La Frantz lage annonsene».
**Why it matters:** It feels like I did something wrong. The order form never asked me to upload the files I said I had.
**What to change:** Ask for files on the summary when the text says «ferdig materiell». On the campaign page, say «Last opp annonsene dere har» as a task, not a yellow warning.
**Tag:** test it

**What I saw:** «Legg inn sporingskoden» and a `<script>` block in the side panel, the day I order.
**Why it matters:** Code is scary for a recruiter. It also shows on Stillinger, as a short number (777845) that is not the campaign number (#129075). Two numbers, same word?
**What to change:** Show only «Send til nettansvarlig» to the customer. Hide the raw code behind «Se kode». Use one name for each number.
**Tag:** test it

**What I saw:** «Kampanje i portalen: http://localhost…» in «Kampanjeoversikt».
**Why it matters:** I am already in the portal. Why do I need its address?
**What to change:** Remove it, or make it a «Kopier lenke» icon by the title.
**Tag:** no-brainer

## 6. Kampanjer vs Stillinger

**What I saw:** Kampanjer says «Kampanjene dere har bestilt» but lists a «Kladd». Stillinger lists the same «Kladd». Where does a draft live?
**Why it matters:** Two lists share the same data, and the link between them is hidden.
**What to change:** This is the open decision in STATUS.md (Stillinger = only jobs without a campaign). Variant B: put Stillinger as the first step inside «Ny kampanje» and drop it from the menu.
**Tag:** test it

**What I saw:** «Connect» in the Stillinger intro.
**Why it matters:** Product name. A new customer does not know it.
**What to change:** «Stillingene hentes automatisk fra rekrutteringssystemet deres.»
**Tag:** no-brainer

## 7. Tilbudskalkulator vs Lagrede pakker

**What I saw:** The calculator has «Lagre søk». Lagrede pakker has «Ny pakke». Both save budget and setup. What is the difference?
**Why it matters:** Two doors to the same room. Which one do I use next time?
**What to change:** Make one: «Lagre som pakke» in the calculator, and show them in one place.
**Tag:** test it

**What I saw:** «Tilbudskalkulator» in the menu. Do I get a «tilbud»? From who?
**Why it matters:** The page itself says «Hva når dere for budsjettet?». That is a better name.
**What to change:** Menu label «Hva får jeg for budsjettet?» or «Beregn rekkevidde».
**Tag:** test it

**What I saw:** Lagrede pakker: Liten, Mellom, Stor. Good names. But what do I **not** get in Liten?
**Why it matters:** Without reach per package, a downgrade is a guess.
**What to change:** Add the expected reach line to each card, the same as the summary («ca. 8–10 trykker «Søk»»).
**Tag:** test it

---

## How to work on the "test it" points
- Do not break the current version. Keep it as variant A.
- Build variant B the way you think is best.
- Do the task yourself in both. Write down what felt natural.
- Now you have an argument for the client: «I tested both, and B is more natural because …».

## Questions for the client
1. [GAP] Is «Bestill kampanjen» binding, or a request that Frantz confirms?
2. [GAP] May the portal pre-tick a paid extra the customer asked for in free text?
3. [GAP] Does «godkjenning» mean the plan, the ads, or both? Who approves which?
4. [GAP] Is the tracking code one number per job (777845) or per campaign (#129075)?
5. [GAP] How does a brand-new customer get an account today?

## Close
- What works: the free-text start that turns into a full summary is strong. The reach estimate («8–10 som trykker «Søk»») next to the budget makes the price feel real. «Slik går det videre» in three steps is clear.
- This is a gut reaction from someone without your context. Test it, do not just obey it.
