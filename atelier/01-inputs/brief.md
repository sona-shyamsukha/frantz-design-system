# Brief — Frantz portal review (adopted project)

- Client: Frantz (recruitment advertising). Vokser does the UX subproject. Handover 30.10, client presentation 3.11.2026.
- Problem: "The service is good. The tool around it is clumsy." Dialogue is spread across email. Ordering is heavy. Targeting is guessing. Reports do not explain the numbers.
- Audience: agencies, partners, HR advisers in public and private sector. ~200 customers. Mostly existing users of business.frantz.no.
- Review scope (Sona, 28.10): Hjem, Ny kampanje, campaign pages (Bestilt, Under arbeid, Aktiv, Avsluttet), report, approval.
- Two lenses (Sona, 28.10): new customers with no campaigns, and existing customers moving from today's portal.
- Constraints: Norwegian UI, WCAG 2.2 AA, no UI libraries, existing Frantz DS tokens (navy primary, amber one key action).
- Research: `research/jamie-insights.md`, `Kunder/Frantz/2-research/`, `losninger.html`.

## Primary user tasks
1. Order a campaign fast, with what they already have (text, files, budget).
2. Find a campaign again by their own name or number.
3. Get approval from a manager or end client.
4. Know what is happening with an order.
5. Show the result to their own client.

## Design principles (from research)
1. Let Frantz choose. Users want less guessing, not more options.
2. Meet the existing habit. Paste, upload, done must still work.
3. One place per campaign. Dialogue, links and approval live with the campaign.
4. Numbers with meaning. Every number needs context.
5. Plain Norwegian. No product names or internal IDs the user does not know.
