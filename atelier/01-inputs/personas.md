# Personas — Frantz portal

Roles come from the interviews (research). Names, ages and details marked [A] are assumptions.

## Mari — agency advisor, existing customer (research: Whydentify, Talentech, 23.09–23.10)
- Context: desk, laptop, 10–15 campaigns a week, several at once. [A: name]
- Goal: order a Tailored campaign by pasting the approved strategy and uploading finished ads.
- Conditions: 1440px desktop, mouse and keyboard.
- Knows: today's business.frantz.no well. Uses her own job number, not Frantz ID.
- Worries: the new form takes longer than pasting. She cannot find her campaign. Dialogue is split.
- Success: order sent in under 3 minutes. Finds it again by her own number.

## Hanne — HR advisor in a municipality, occasional buyer (research: 28.09)
- Context: a few campaigns a year. Must get the manager to approve price and ad first. [A: name]
- Goal: see price, channels and a preview, then send it to the manager for approval.
- Conditions: laptop, sometimes phone.
- Knows: little about channels or Frantz terms.
- Worries: hidden costs, choosing the wrong thing.
- Success: manager approves from a link, then she orders.

## Jonas — new customer, first login (inferred, no interview) [A]
- Context: got access after a sales meeting. No campaigns. No onboarding.
- Goal: understand what to do first and place a first order.
- Conditions: desktop.
- Knows: nothing about Autopublish, Tailored or Frantz AI.
- Worries: committing money without knowing what happens next.
- Success: knows what Frantz does, starts a campaign, knows Frantz reviews before publishing.

## Sigrid — screen-reader user (accessibility persona) [A]
- Context: HR advisor, blind, uses VoiceOver and keyboard only.
- Goal: check status of an order and open the report.
- Conditions: keyboard only, screen reader, 1440px.
- Success: reaches status and report without a mouse, every control is named.

## Ola — branch manager in a hurry, on the phone (mobile persona; research: Hyr 07.10)
- Context: between meetings, iPhone, one hand. [A: name]
- Goal: check that a campaign is live and copy the report link for a client.
- Conditions: 390px, touch, 4G.
- Success: done in under a minute, no zooming.
