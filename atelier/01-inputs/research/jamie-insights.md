# Insights from Jamie transcripts (distilled 28.10.2026)

Sources: Jamie transcripts and summaries, 03.09–23.10.2026. Full transcripts read for the two 23.10 interviews.
**No meeting tested the prototype.** Every external participant is an existing user. New-customer findings are inferred and weak.
Jamie garbles names: "Frans" = Frantz, "Taylor" = Tailored.

## Meetings
- Campaign designer, integration partner (Talentech/Jobbnorge), 23.10: orders are quick ("vi har det jo i fingrene"). Slow work happens before Frantz. Does not want another tool to build ads in.
- Campaign coordinator, same partner, 23.10: pastes the whole approved strategy into Tailored's free-text field. Loves the report. Wants share links stored in the portal.
- Branch manager, recruitment agency (Hyr), 07.10: ~90% of roles go through Frantz. Product names mean nothing to her.
- Internal status, Vokser, 01.10: synthesis of 5 interviews.
- Recruitment specialist, 2-person firm, 28.09: wants benchmarks and one place per assignment for dialogue and approvals.
- HR adviser, municipality, 28.09: uses Autopublish or email. Would self-serve with channels, live price and preview.
- Strategic adviser, Whydentify, 24.09: portal feels "2010-tallet". Report is key but hard for non-marketers.
- Partner, Headley, 23.09: dashboard "gir meg absolutt 0". Wants search → Bestill → wizard, and an API.
- Media adviser, Whydentify, 23.09: email-first. Replies arrive as separate threads.
- Frantz admins ×2, 11.09: most orders still arrive by email. 125-character ad text limit.
- Frantz management, 07.09 and 10.09: segments, pricing, approval link (Frantz AI only).

## A) New customers (inferred)
- Product names (Autopublish, Tailored, Frantz AI) block understanding, even for experienced users. Hyr, 07.10.
- No clear first action today. Headley, 23.09: "minner meg mer om en meny enn en knapp".
- Category and job-title pickers force guessing ("CFO" returns nothing). Hyr 07.10; Whydentify 24.09.
- A low-frequency buyer wants web-shop transparency: channels, live price, preview. Municipal HR, 28.09.
- New accounts get access straight after a sales meeting, with no onboarding. Frantz management, 10.09.

## B) Existing customers moving from business.frantz.no
- Habit: paste everything into one free-text field, upload finished artwork, let Frantz do the rest. Coordinator 23.10: "limer vi jo bare inn… hele den kampanjestrategien". A step form risks feeling like more work.
- Fixed packages alone won't fit. Coordinator 23.10: "det vil alltid være avvik fra de pakkene". Split periods are free text. Hyr wants its own presets.
- Customer approval happens before Frantz, in their own tools, never after launch. Designer 23.10: "Nei, ikke etter at den er gått live." Øyvind 28.09 does several email rounds.
- They find campaigns by their own ID (Jobbnorge ID, job number), not Frantz ID. Coordinator 23.10: "det er jo Fran sin ID og den har ikke vi".
- Share links (Meta, LinkedIn) live in email. Want them on the campaign page next to the report link.
- Order confirmation and "live" emails are valued. Coordinator 23.10: "veldig sånn grei kontrollfunksjon".
- Two message actions («Kundedialog», «Forespør endringer») confuse, so they use email. Coordinator 23.10: "hvorfor er det to…".
- Autopublish users value doing it themselves cheaply. Want >125 characters, better cropping, video.

## Both
- Report loved by the partner, too technical for non-marketers. Want impressions, trend, fixed channel colours.
- Numbers need context: benchmarks, applicants per krone, targeting by channel.
- Frantz AI budget suggestions feel too high (19–22k vs. typical 8–12k).
- Want several locations or categories.
- Human advice is valued. Complex roles still get a phone call. Keep a visible contact route.

## Weak evidence
- 8 external users. Several are owner-affiliated or partners. No first-time customer and no end-client approver interviewed.
- Self-service vs. "let Frantz do it" splits users. Email is fine for some, scattered for others.
